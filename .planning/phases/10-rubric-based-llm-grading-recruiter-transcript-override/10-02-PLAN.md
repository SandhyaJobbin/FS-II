<objective>
Persist the rubric-grading output. This plan (a) creates the `GradingTranscripts` sheet in the same lazy-init convention as the other four sheets, (b) adds an `UngradedCount` column to `Attempts` (column O = 15), (c) rewires `gradeAndFinalizeAttempt` in `backend/AsyncGrading.gs` to call `evaluateWithRubric` (from plan 10-01), aggregate scores under the denominator-excludes-ungraded policy (RESEARCH.md A2), and batch-write transcript rows, and (d) introduces the `effectiveVerdict` resolver that codifies the OverrideVerdict > Verdict > IsCorrect precedence to prevent the double-source-of-truth drift described in RESEARCH.md Pitfall 2.

Purpose: This is the persistence-and-aggregation half of Wave 1. It depends on plan 10-01 (which defines `evaluateWithRubric` and the rubric block on QUESTIONS). Splitting the wiring here from the definition in 10-01 keeps each plan's diff scoped to a single sheet-schema concern (10-01 = grading logic + question data; 10-02 = persistence + scoring loop; 10-03 = override endpoint) so context stays under 50% per plan.

Output: A rubric-graded pipeline that persists transcripts and honestly counts ungraded answers, all still callable from the same 5-minute `processGradingQueue` trigger installed in Phase 9.
</objective>
